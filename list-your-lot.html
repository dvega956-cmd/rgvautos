FAQ
Common questions
Do I need a website to list on RGV Autos?
No. RGV Autos is your online presence. You don't need your own website — we give you a full dealer profile page, your own listing section, and a direct contact form for buyers. Many of our dealers use RGV Autos as their only online presence.
Can I manage my own inventory, or do I have to rely on you?
Every plan includes your own dealer dashboard — a simple login where you can add new vehicles, update prices, edit details, upload photos straight from your phone, and mark cars as sold the moment they're gone. Sold vehicles disappear from the public listing automatically. You're never waiting on us to make a change. During the trial, we work with you directly to set things up.
Do I have to pay for photography, or can I upload my own photos?
You upload your own photos — just snap a few shots of each vehicle with your phone and share them with us. During the trial, we'll handle the upload setup for you. It's simple and free.
What happens after my free trial ends?
After 3 months, your trial transitions to a paid plan — Standard at $59/month or Featured at $99/month. We'll reach out before your trial ends to discuss your options. If you choose not to continue, your listings are simply removed with no charge and no hard feelings. / Después de 3 meses, su prueba pasa a un plan de pago — Estándar a $59/mes o Destacado a $99/mes.
How do buyers contact me — does RGV Autos take a cut of the sale?
Never. When a buyer clicks "Contact" on your listing, their message goes directly to your email and phone number. We are not a middleman in the sale. We charge a flat monthly fee and you keep 100% of every sale you make.
I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need this?
Facebook is great for visibility but terrible for car shopping. Buyers can't search your inventory by price, year, or mileage. Your posts disappear from feeds within hours. RGV Autos gives buyers a searchable, organized catalog of your inventory that's live 24/7 — and every listing is optimized to show up in Google searches for local used cars in Brownsville.
How long does it take to go live?
During the trial, we aim to get your first listings live within 48 hours of your signup. Just share your vehicle list and a few phone photos of each car and we'll take it from there.
Is this platform actually live, or just a concept?
The platform is real and live at rgvautos.vercel.app — you can browse it right now. We're in a founding dealer trial phase, meaning we're onboarding our first 5 dealerships before launching the full self-service version. You're getting in early, for free, and your feedback directly shapes what gets built next.
