FAQ
Common questions
Do I need a website to list on RGV Autos?
No. RGV Autos is your online presence. You don't need your own website — we give you a full dealer profile page, your own listing section, and a direct contact form for buyers. Many of our dealers use RGV Autos as their only online presence.
Can I manage my own inventory, or do I have to rely on you?
Every plan includes your own dealer dashboard — a simple login where you can add new vehicles, update prices, edit details, upload photos straight from your phone, and mark cars as sold the moment they're gone. Sold vehicles disappear from the public listing automatically. You're never waiting on us to make a change. That said, if you'd rather hand it off entirely, our photo add-on service means we come to your lot and handle everything for you.
Do I have to pay for photography, or can I upload my own photos?
Photography is completely optional and never required. Every plan lets you upload your own photos directly from your phone — just snap a few shots of the car and attach them when adding a listing. It takes about 2 minutes. If you'd prefer we handle it, we offer a one-time setup session ($100 for up to 20 vehicles) or a monthly refresh add-on ($50/month) where we visit your lot regularly and keep everything updated. Most dealers start by uploading their own photos and add the service later once they see the results.
What happens after my free trial ends?
After 3 months, your trial transitions to a paid plan — Standard at $79/month or Featured at $129/month. We'll reach out before your trial ends to discuss your options. If you choose not to continue, your listings are simply removed with no charge and no hard feelings.
How do buyers contact me — does RGV Autos take a cut of the sale?
Never. When a buyer clicks "Contact" on your listing, their message goes directly to your email and phone number. We are not a middleman in the sale. We charge a flat monthly fee and you keep 100% of every sale you make.
I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need this?
Facebook is great for visibility but terrible for car shopping. Buyers can't search your inventory by price, year, or mileage. Your posts disappear from feeds within hours. RGV Autos gives buyers a searchable, organized catalog of your inventory that's live 24/7 — and every listing is optimized to show up in Google searches for local used cars in Brownsville.
How long does it take to go live?
If you manage your own inventory, you can be live the same day you sign up — just log into your dashboard, add your first few vehicles with photos, and they're published immediately. If you'd like us to handle the initial setup and photography, we aim for 48 hours from signup to live listings.
